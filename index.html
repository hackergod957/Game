User Manual

Objective: Heal all 10 plants to unlock the Disease Demon boss. Defeat the boss by answering 5 questions correctly in a row.

How to Play:

  • Click on a plant photo that matches the symptom shown above.
  • Type the plant's scientific name (case‑insensitive, dots ignored).
  • Press Treat (or Enter) to submit.
  • After a correct answer, the game automatically moves to a new plant (1.5 sec delay).

Hearts & Health: Start with 3 hearts. Wrong answers cost 1 heart. Gain hearts by:

  • Healing a new plant (first time correct).
  • Streak of 3 correct answers (1 heart, max 3).
  • ⚠️ If you get a wrong answer and then use the displayed correct name, you will NOT gain a heart (only mana & streak).

Mana & Spells: Earn 1 mana per correct answer (max 10). Spells:

  • Reveal (4 mana): Highlights the correct plant.
  • Lightning (2 mana): Disables one random wrong plant for the round.
  • Heal (4 mana): Restores one heart (if below max).

Levels: Every 2 unique plants healed increases your level (up to 5). Your hat changes! 🧢→🎩→👑→🪖→🧙

Streak: Consecutive correct answers increase your streak. Every 3rd correct gives a bonus heart.

Shuffle: Rearranges the four plant cards – useful if you're stuck.

Hint: Shows the common name and first letter of the scientific name (no penalty).

Journal: Track which plants you've healed.

Sound: Mute/unmute with the sound button.

Game Over: If hearts reach zero, you die. Click reincarnate to restart.
